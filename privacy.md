# Privacy Policy for Character Builder

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Character Builder (the "App") handles your data. It is written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It applies to the App on the Apple App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

## 1. Data Controller

The data controller is Light Cup Pix Lab, Padova, Italy (the studio that develops the App "Character Builder").

Contact: lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com

## 2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The App has no account and no sign-in, and we (the developer) never receive the content you create.

What you write in the App (projects, characters, their details and custom fields, family and bond threads, project notes and saved project templates) is your content. It is stored only on your devices and, if you turn it on, in your own private iCloud, as described in "Where Your Data Is Stored".

Please note: free-text fields (names, roles, notes, custom fields) are stored as you type them. They stay under your control, but avoid entering sensitive details about real people.

## 3. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or camera content, biometric data, advertising identifiers or device identifiers. The App does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, tracking or push-notification software, and it contains no third-party SDKs. We do not collect or store payment card details or any payment instrument data.

## 4. Where Your Data Is Stored

- On your device. Your content is stored by the App on your iPhone, iPad or Mac and is protected by the device's data protection.
- In your private iCloud (optional). If iCloud sync is on, the same content is stored in your private CloudKit database (container "iCloud.com.characterbuilder.app"), so it appears on your other Apple devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Only your Apple Account can read that database; we cannot access it. Apple's own privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings, then "iCloud sync"; a full copy then stays on the device.
- Preferences (language, appearance, map style, haptic feedback) are stored only on your device.

## 5. Legal Bases (Art. 6 GDPR)

Because we do not collect or receive personal data, we do not process your data on any legal basis. Your content is processed only on your devices and in your own iCloud account, under your control.

## 6. Your Rights (Art. 15-22 GDPR)

You keep full control of your content directly in the App:

- Access and portability. Use Settings, then "Export my data" to save all your projects, characters, threads, notes and templates as a JSON file. You can bring it back with Settings, then "Import a backup".
- Rectification. Edit your projects and characters directly in the App.
- Erasure. Use Settings, then "Delete all data" (you confirm by typing the word DELETE) to remove every project and template from the device and from your private iCloud database. This cannot be undone. Deleting the App removes the local copy.

You can contact us at lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com with any question, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Your content stays on your devices (and in your private iCloud, if sync is on) until you delete it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side.

## 8. Sub-processors

We do not use sub-processors. The App relies only on services provided by Apple:

- Apple iCloud (CloudKit): optional sync of your content in your private iCloud database.
- Apple App Store: processes subscription and in-app purchases and billing.

## 9. Data Location and International Transfers

We do not transfer your data anywhere. If you use iCloud sync, your content is stored by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple's terms and privacy policy.

## 10. Children

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, children included.

## 11. Payments

The App offers an optional paid auto-renewable subscription ("Character Builder Pro") and optional one-time purchases of character packs. All purchases are processed and billed entirely by the Apple App Store, under Apple's terms and privacy policy. The App checks on your device whether a subscription is active or a pack has been purchased. We do not receive or store your payment card or any payment instrument data, and we do not receive your identity from Apple.

## 12. Security

Your content is protected by your device's data protection and, if you use iCloud sync, by Apple's iCloud security. The App does not send your content to any server of ours.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The new version will be published at this address and the "Last updated" date above will be revised.

## 14. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Light Cup Pix Lab at lightcuppixlab.dev@gmail.com.
